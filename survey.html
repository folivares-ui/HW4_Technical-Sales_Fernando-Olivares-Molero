Your turn

What did you learn?

These questions can be used to survey students after they visit the site. Try them yourself: rate each statement from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree).

1.Before visiting this website, I felt confident about my business dining etiquette.

2.After visiting this website, I feel more confident about my business dining etiquette.

3.I can explain which utensils to use first and where the bread plate and glasses belong (“outside-in” and “BMW”).

4.I know what to do with my phone and when to start talking business during a meal.

5.The interactive place-setting picture helped me understand the table.

6.The games and the video lesson made learning the material more enjoyable.

7.The website was easy to navigate and easy to read.

8.I would feel comfortable attending a business lunch or dinner after using this site.

9.What is the single most useful thing you learned about business dining?

10.What topic would you like more information about, and how could this site be improved?
